## 🥗 Core Clinical Protocols & Targets

### 1. Chronic Kidney Disease (Stages 3–4 Non-Dialysis)
* **Sodium Target**: $< 1,500\text{ mg/day}$ (strict blood pressure and glomerular hyperfiltration protection).
* **Potassium Target**: $< 2,000\text{ mg/day}$ (monitored per serum potassium bloodwork).
* **Phosphorus**: Strictly avoid inorganic chemical polyphosphates (processed foods/dark sodas).
* **Protein Ceiling**: $0.55 - 0.60\text{ g/kg body weight/day}$ (KDOQI 2020 Clinical Guidelines).

### 2. Type 2 Diabetes & Insulin Resistance
* **Carbohydrate Strategy**: Complex, low-glycemic carbs ($< 45\text{g net carbs per meal}$).
* **Fiber Floor**: Minimum $35\text{g/day}$ to attenuate postprandial glucose excursions.
* **Glycemic Load Focus**: Low glycemic load pairing (soluble fiber + healthy monounsaturated fat buffer).

### 3. Congestive Heart Failure & Hypertension (DASH Sodium Restriction)
* **Sodium Ceiling**: $< 1,500\text{ mg/day}$ strict.
* **Potassium-to-Sodium Ratio**: Optimized to $> 2:1$ via leafy greens, squash, and potassium-dense non-citrus fruits.
* **AHA Heart-Check Compliance**: Saturated fat $< 6\%$ of total daily calories.
